The observed prior mini-app failure example is *outcome evidence tied to its captured revision*, not a proof that all problems originated in the original Skill. Possible mechanisms include incomplete diagnosis, missing platform knowledge, agent implementation quality and insufficient rendered/device verification. This motivates the new probes but does not establish that the candidate improves behavior until paired tests run.
